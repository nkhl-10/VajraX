# VAJRAX: Primary User Flow & App Navigation

This document defines the exact step-by-step user experience, from the first launch to daily execution and analytics review.

---

## 1. Onboarding Flow (First Launch)
*Objective: Short, personalized, and progressive. No endless configuration.*

1. **Welcome**: *"Choose the life you want to build. VAJRAX turns it into today's actions."* -> `[Begin]`
2. **Authentication**: Google, Apple, or Guest. (Zero friction).
3. **Choose Your Life Path**: High Performance, Self-Mastery, Scholar, Wealth Builder, Balanced Life, Creator, Mindful Life, Purpose-Driven, Custom.
4. **Personalization (Max 2 questions)**:
   - *Q1*: What are you trying to improve most right now? (Select 3: Deep Focus, Physical Discipline, Self-Respect, etc.)
   - *Q2*: What's your primary role? (Developer, Founder, Creator, Student, Executive, etc.)
5. **Wake / Sleep Boundaries**: Wake time (e.g. 6:00 AM) & Sleep window (e.g. 10:30 PM).
6. **Build My System**: VAJRAX generates the initial 5 practices (3 Core, 2 Supporting) mapped to foundational principles.
7. **Minimum Goal Setup**: Normal target vs Minimum target (e.g., Deep Work: 60m normal / 15m minimum).
8. **Enter System**: System activation complete.

---

## 2. HOME / TODAY: The Single-Core Execution Loop
*Objective: Home is NOT an overwhelming checklist. It answers: "Where am I right now + What is my single next action?"*

### 2.1 DASHBOARD (30% visual attention)
- **Consistency Score**: Overall consistency rate (e.g., 85% with `↑ 12% this month`).
- **Circadian Progress Ring**: Smooth donut indicating active-day progression.
- **Stat Pods**: 3 pods (Today: `8/10`, Week: `85%`, Consistency: `92%`).

### 2.2 TIMELINE FOCUS (70% visual attention)
Categorized strictly into three distinct tiers:
1. **✓ COMPLETED**: Tasks already finished today with clear filled checkmark badges.
2. **▶ NOW (Active Focus Card)**:
   - The single most important task right now.
   - Distinct highlighted card with 1-tap `[Start]` (or Timer) and 1-tap `✓ Complete`.
3. **○ NEXT**: Immediate upcoming practices for later in the morning/afternoon.
4. **○ LATER**: Evening routines and reflections.

---

## 3. The 2–3s Rewarding Completion & Optional Evidence Loop
When an action is completed:
1. **Instant Tap**: User taps `✓` on the NOW card or notification.
2. **Instant Feedback (0ms - 200ms)**:
   - Checkmark circle fills with smooth ease-out.
   - Subtle tactile haptic pulse.
   - Progress ring and consistency percentages increment immediately.
   - Subtle non-intrusive prompt: `✓ Done  ·  [+ Add evidence]`
3. **Optional Evidence (Tiered)**:
   - *Normal*: No action needed; focus advances to the NEXT practice immediately.
   - *Important*: Tap `+ Add evidence` to quickly attach a photo/metric.
   - *Reflection-Worthy*: Tap to open quick reflection sheet (1-tap rating + optional note).
   - Zero nagging, zero forced popups.

---

## 4. REPORT / GROW: Principles & Pattern Discovery
*Objective: Answer "Which part of my life system is becoming stronger?" and "Why do I succeed or fail?"*

1. **Overall Completion & Score Donut**: 78% score with weekly delta (`↑ 5%`).
2. **7-Day Workload Bar Chart**: Daily completed vs planned ratios (`M: 6/8, T: 7/8, W: 5/8, T: 8/8, F: 7/8, S: 4/8, S: 6/8`).
3. **Consistency Pods**: Current streak (`12 days`) & Best consistency (`21 days`).
4. **Your Principles Breakdown**:
   - Physical Discipline: `91%` (Strongest)
   - Deep Work: `84%`
   - Self-Respect: `78%`
   - Continuous Learning: `72%`
   - Daily Reflection: `63%` (Needs attention)
5. **Pattern Discovery Layer (Transparent Intelligence)**:
   - **Raw Data**: "18 sessions analyzed (14 Morning / 4 Afternoon)."
   - **VAJRAX Interpretation**: "Deep Work is 34% more likely to succeed before 11:00 AM."
   - **Interactive "Why?" Button**: Tapping opens the transparent breakdown modal showing hourly completion rates and fatigue correlations.

---

## 5. Main Navigation (5 Tabs)
1. **TODAY**: Single-Core execution (NOW / NEXT / LATER).
2. **CALENDAR**: Weekly schedule matrix & consistency heatmap.
3. **DISCOVER**: Curated Life Paths and Book-to-Life templates.
4. **REPORT**: Overall completion, Principle Strength, and Transparent Pattern Discovery.
5. **PROFILE**: Identity, Active Path, Supabase sync status, and theme controls.
