# VAJRAX: Primary User Flow & App Navigation

This document defines the exact step-by-step user experience, from the first launch to daily execution.

---

## 1. Onboarding Flow (First Launch)
*Objective: Short, personalized, and progressive. No endless configuration.*

1.  **Welcome**: "VAJRAX: Build. Discipline. Become. A self-directed system for the life you want to build." -> [Begin]
2.  **Authentication**: Google, Apple, or Guest. (No friction).
3.  **Choose Your Life Path**: Visual cards for Apex, Sigma, Scholar, Wealth, Dharma, Creator, Balanced. (Can change later).
4.  **Personalization (Max 2 questions)**:
    *   *Q1*: What are you trying to improve most right now? (Select 3: Health, Career, Focus, etc.)
    *   *Q2*: What's your primary role? (Student, Developer, Entrepreneur, etc.)
5.  **Wake / Sleep Boundaries**: When does your day begin? (e.g., 6:00 AM) When does it end?
6.  **Build My Day**: VAJRAX generates the first routine (Max 5 practices: 3 Core, 2 Supporting).
7.  **Minimum Goal Setup**: Set Normal Target vs Minimum Target (e.g., Deep Work: 60m normal / 10m minimum).
8.  **Confirm System**: "Your system is ready. Enter VAJRAX."

---

## 2. HOME (The "Living Timeline" Dashboard)
*Objective: Home is NOT a checklist. It is a calm command center. "Where am I? + What should I do now?"*

The Home screen is strictly divided into two sections:

### 2.1 DASHBOARD (30% visual attention)
Answers: *How am I doing?*
*   **Progress**: Global consistency percentage (e.g., 78%).
*   **Today's Status**: 3/5 completed.
*   **Stats**: Focus time, active Path, day count.

### 2.2 TODAY (70% visual attention)
Answers: *What should I do now?*
This section is **Time-Aware**. It changes dynamically based on the clock.
*   **✓ COMPLETED**: Practices already done today.
*   **▶ NOW (One Focus)**: The currently due practice presented as a prominent Glassmorphism card. Includes the `[Start]` button.
*   **○ NEXT**: Upcoming practices for later in the day.

---

## 3. The 3-Second Execution Loop
When a practice is due:
1.  **Notification**: "Deep Work starts now." -> Actions: `[Done]`, `[Minimum]`.
2.  **In-App Action**: Tap `[Start]` or Quick Complete (`✓ Done`, `½ Min`, `⏭ Skip`).
3.  **Evidence**: After completion, optionally attach Photo, Note, or Voice. (Never forced journaling).

---

## 4. Main Navigation (5 Tabs)
1.  **TODAY**: What should I do now? (The living timeline).
2.  **PATH**: Who am I becoming? (Identity, philosophy, routine editing).
3.  **LEARN**: What am I learning? (Book-to-Life system, insights).
4.  **GROW**: What evidence am I building? (Evidence logs, long-term history).
5.  **REVIEW**: What am I learning about myself? (Weekly patterns, Bheda recommendations).
