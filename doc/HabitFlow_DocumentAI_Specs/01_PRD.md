# HabitFlow — Product Requirements Document

## 1. Product Overview
HabitFlow is a personalized daily routine, habit tracking, and personal growth application. Users select a default routine template, customize it, track habits every day, and review weekly/monthly progress.

## 2. Product Vision
Help users convert intentions into consistent daily actions with the minimum possible tracking friction.

## 3. Core Product Loop
Select Template → Personalize Routine → Daily Plan → One-Tap Check-in → Progress Analysis → Weekly Review → Adjust Routine → Repeat.

## 4. Target Users
- People building daily routines.
- Students and learners.
- Professionals managing routines and productivity.
- Fitness/wellness users.
- Users who currently use paper or spreadsheet habit trackers.
- Users who want structured personal growth without complex productivity systems.

## 5. Core Value Proposition
- Ready-made routine templates.

- Time-based daily schedule.
- One-tap habit completion.
- Android home-screen quick actions.

- Goal → routine → habit relationship.

## 8. Differentiators
### Flexible Routine
Users can move, skip, snooze, or reschedule habits without corrupting historical data.

### Recovery Mode
A missed habit does not automatically become a punitive failure experience. Users can complete today, reschedule, skip, or record a reason.

### Habit Insights
Identify completion patterns, difficult habits, preferred times, and repeated missed reasons.

### Adaptive Routine
After sufficient history, suggest schedule or target adjustments based on actual completion behavior.

### Today's Plan
Show what is happening now and what comes next rather than presenting only a flat checklist.

### Android Widget
Allow users to see progress, current/next habit, and complete common actions without opening the app.

## 10. Non-Goals for MVP
- Social network.
- Public creator marketplace.
- Complex AI coaching.
- Wear OS.
- Multi-device cloud synchronization.
- Paid template marketplace.
- Advanced health integrations.

## 11. Product Principles
1. Tracking must be faster than opening a spreadsheet.
2. Missed habits must not create unnecessary guilt.
3. Historical records must never be silently rewritten.
4. Analytics must be explainable.
5. Users control their schedule and data.
6. Default templates are immutable source definitions.
7. A user's tracker is a personal copy of a template.
