# User Flow Document: VAJRAX

This document outlines the core user journeys within VAJRAX, focusing on Progressive Autonomy and the Sāma, Dāma, Daṇḍa, Bheda Intervention Engine.

## 1. Onboarding: Defining the Path (Stage 1)
   - **App**: "What kind of life are you building?"
   - **User**: Selects core values (e.g., Self-respect, Competence).
   - **App**: Creates a starting path focused purely on *Self-Trust*.

## 2. Daily Execution: The "Don't Make Me Think" UX
   - **User**: Opens the app.
   - **App**: Displays a clean UI. "Your Next Move: 💻 Deep Work — 60 min [START]"
   - **User**: Completes the session.
   - **App**: "Kept your word." (Optional 📷 Evidence or 📝 Thought).

## 3. The Intervention Flow: Handling Resistance
When a user repeatedly fails a habit (e.g., Waking up at 6:00 AM), VAJRAX escalates through the behavioral engine rather than nagging.

1. **SĀMA (Align)**
   - **App**: "Why 6 AM? Your morning is for your future self. Let's make it easier. Just wake up and drink water."
   - *If resistance continues...*

2. **DĀMA (Reward/Evidence)**
   - **App (After a few successes)**: "7 mornings kept. You've created evidence that you can keep your own commitments."
   - *If user starts failing again...*

3. **DAṆḌA (Reality Check)**
   - **App**: "Reality Check. Planned: 6:00 AM. Actual average: 7:18 AM. Your current schedule doesn't support your goal." (No guilt, just truth).
   - *If user is stuck...*

4. **BHEDA (Pattern Discovery)**
   - **VAJRAX Analytics**: Detects that sleep before 11 PM yields 82% success, after 12 AM yields 29%.
   - **App**: "The problem isn't waking up. It's your previous night's routine. Recommendation: Add 10:30 PM Digital Shutdown."

## 4. The "Learn → Apply" Flow (Knowledge Application)
   - **User**: Logs a book insight on the 📚 **LEARN** tab.
   - **App**: Prompts translating it into a Personal Principle and a Daily Action.
   - **App**: Starts a 7-day experiment. User reflects on Day 7 ("Did this work?").

## 5. Long-Term Flow: Progressive Autonomy
The app changes how it speaks to the user over time:
1. **Beginner**: "Do this." (High guidance)
2. **Developing**: "Try this." (Suggesting 7-day experiments)
3. **Experienced**: "Here's what your data suggests." (Bheda pattern discovery)
4. **Advanced**: "You decide." (User builds custom routines)
5. **Mature**: "You know what to do." (VAJRAX becomes a silent mirror)
