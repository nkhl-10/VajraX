# VAJRAX Privacy Policy

Effective 30 September 2026

## In short

VAJRAX works offline. Everything you enter stays on your device. There are no accounts, servers, ads, analytics or trackers, and the app has no permission to use the internet, so it cannot send your data anywhere.

## What the app stores on your device

Android keeps this in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read.

- Your name and email, only if you add them
- Your goals, wake-up time and preferences
- Routines, habits, check-ins, notes, skip reasons, goals and reflections
- Reminder, widget and display settings

## What we collect

Nothing. We never receive, sell or share your data, and it is not used for advertising or profiling.

## Permissions

- Notifications: to show the habit reminders you turn on
- Run at start-up: to restore your reminders after the phone restarts

The app does not ask for location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or your files.

## Device backup

If backup is on for your phone, Android may include VAJRAX data in your device backup so you can restore it on a new phone. Google handles these backups under your Google account settings; we cannot access them. You can turn backup off in Android Settings.

## Reminders, widgets and exports

Reminders and widgets can show habit names on the lock screen and home screen. To keep them private, choose Generic or No details in Profile › Notifications, and hide names on the widget.

An export file holds your full history and notes. It is saved only where you choose; keep it and share it with care.

## Your choices

You can see and change all of your data in the app at any time.

- Profile › Export my data saves a full copy as a JSON file
- Profile › Delete all data erases everything from this device
- Uninstalling the app also removes its data from the device

## Children

VAJRAX is not directed at children under 13. It does not collect data from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If a future version adds a feature that sends data off your device, such as cloud sync, this policy will be updated with a new effective date before that version is released.

## Contact

Use the developer contact details on the VAJRAX page in Google Play.
