# Project VAJRAX — Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## Global Positioning
> **VAJRAX is a Self-Directed Life OS that helps people turn philosophy, books, psychology, goals, and personal values into daily behavior.**
**Build. Discipline. Become.**

VAJRAX is engineered for a **global audience**, synthesizing **Stoicism, Modern Psychology, Behavioral Science, Vedic philosophy, Buddhism, and Classical Philosophy**. It provides a culturally inclusive, self-directed operating system for intentional living.

The universal product loop is:
`Philosophy / Science → Principles → Life Path → Identity & Values → Goals → Daily System → Practices → Challenges → Execution → Evidence → Reflection → Pattern Detection → Adaptation → Personal Growth`

---

## 1. The 5 Major Systems
VAJRAX is built around five core pillars:
1. **LIFE OS**: Who do I want to become? (Health, Mind, Career, Wealth, Relationships, Purpose)
2. **KNOWLEDGE**: What do I believe / learn? (Books, philosophies, behavioral research)
3. **EXECUTION**: What do I do today? (Time-aware Living Timeline, 3-second completion)
4. **BEHAVIOR ENGINE**: Why do I succeed/fail? (Least-force intervention: Align, Reward, Reality, Pattern)
5. **GROWTH**: Who am I becoming? (Evidence of Character, Growth Dimensions)

---

## 2. Global Life Paths (The "Who")
Users choose or customize a path defining their direction without rigid dogmas:
*   **⚡ High Performance**: Deep focus, physical vigor, competence, and relentless execution.
*   **🗿 Self-Mastery**: Self-respect, emotional discipline, strong boundaries, and autonomy.
*   **🧠 Scholar**: Reading, rigorous learning, intellectual depth, and critical thinking.
*   **💰 Wealth Builder**: High-value skills, career growth, financial discipline, and long-term leverage.
*   **🌿 Balanced Life**: Holistic health, deep relationships, work-life equilibrium, and calmness.
*   **🎨 Creator**: Deep craft, consistent creative output, focus blocks, and experimentation.
*   **🧘 Mindful Life**: Awareness, emotional regulation, presence, and intentional reflection.
*   **🕉 Purpose-Driven**: Core values, duty, community contribution, and meaningful impact.
*   **🛠 Custom Life**: Fully personalized path designed by the user.

---

## 3. Knowledge Engine (Book → Life System)
VAJRAX clearly distinguishes between **Philosophical Teachings**, **Book Ideas**, and **Evidence-Based Behavioral Science**.
`Idea / Tradition → Practical Application → 7-Day Experiment → Evidence Capture → Micro-Reflection → Adaptation`

---

## 4. Internationalization & Cultural Neutrality
*   **Language & Typography**: English first, localization-ready strings.
*   **Flexible Time Systems**: 12h/24h support, customizable wake/sleep windows (supports early birds, night shifts, and flexible schedules).
*   **Unit Agnostic**: Metric & imperial units.
*   **Universal Home**: 
    - **Dashboard (30%)**: *How am I doing?*
    - **Today (70%)**: *What should I do now?*

---

## 5. Execution & Behavioral Rules
*   **Zero Nagging**: Silent by default. No shame-based notifications or manipulation.
*   **Progressive Autonomy**: Level 1 (Guided) → Level 3 (Adaptive) → Level 5 (Silent mirror).
*   **The 3-Second Rule**: `✓ Done`, `½ Minimum`, `⏭ Skip` in 1 tap.
*   **Evidence System**: Replace arbitrary percentages with real qualitative & quantitative evidence.
*   **Zero Overhead**: Logging must never take more effort than doing the habit itself.
