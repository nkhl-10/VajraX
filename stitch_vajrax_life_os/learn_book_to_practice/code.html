Deep Work

Cal Newport • 2016

KEY IDEA

"Professional activities performed in a state of distraction-free concentration that push your cognitive capabilities to their limit."

psychology Personal Interpretation

My current environment is hostile to deep work. I treat shallow tasks (email, slack) as primary work, rather than administrative overhead. To achieve mastery, I need to restructure my day to defend at least 90 minutes of unbroken focus before external demands intrude.

bolt Convert to Practice 7-Day Experiment

No-Phone Morning

Keep phone in a different room until 9:00 AM. Dedicate the first 90 minutes of the day entirely to the most cognitively demanding task on the docket.
