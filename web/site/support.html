Support

I forgot my password

In the app, open Profile › Backup & sync › Sign in and tap Forgot password?. We email a link that works once, for 30 minutes.

My changes don't show on my other device

Changes sync when the device is online. Open Profile › Backup & sync and tap Sync now. If it says "Waiting for a connection", your changes are safe on that device and will go up when it is back online.

Do I need an account?

No. VAJRAX works fully offline without one. An account only adds backup and sync, including the web app.

Android app

VAJRAX for Android is coming to Google Play. Until then, the web app works in any modern browser.

Delete my data

To remove data from one device, use Profile › Delete all data in the app. To delete your account and everything stored with it, use Delete account.

Contact

Use the developer contact details on the VAJRAX page in Google Play.
