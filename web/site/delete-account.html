Delete your account

This permanently deletes your VAJRAX account and everything backed up with it: routines, habits, check-ins, notes, goals, reflections and your templates. Our database backups expire within 7 days. It can't be undone.

Your phone keeps working: the habits on it stay there until you remove them in the app. You can also delete the account in the app under Profile › Backup & sync › Delete account.

Forgot it? Use "Forgot password?" in the app to get a reset link first.
